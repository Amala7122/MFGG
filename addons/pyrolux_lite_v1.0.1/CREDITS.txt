PYROLUX - credits and provenance

- All GDScript code and shaders in this pack were written specifically for PYROLUX,
  with the help of an AI coding assistant. Nothing was copied from godotshaders.com,
  GitHub, other asset packs or tutorials. Techniques are standard, first-principles
  graphics methods: integer hashing (PCG), value noise and fBm, domain warping,
  camera-facing billboards and ribbons expanded in the vertex shader, closed-form
  ballistic motion with drag, cell (Voronoi) noise, depth-buffer soft particles.
- No textures or images are used by the effects. Everything is computed in shaders.
- No AI-generated images, audio or third-party art are included.
- Editor icons (addons/pyrolux_fx/icons/*.svg) were drawn as hand-written SVG code.
- Demo scenes are built from Godot primitive meshes and polygons in code.
- Demo fonts: Godot's built-in default font.
- Made with Godot Engine (MIT license, https://godotengine.org/license). Godot is not
  included in this pack.
