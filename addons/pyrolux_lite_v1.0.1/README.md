# PYROLUX Lite - Free Fire & Explosion VFX for Godot 4

A free sample of PYROLUX: a procedural campfire (`PyroluxFire3D`) and a ground explosion
(`PyroluxExplosion3D`) for Godot 4.4+. Works on Forward+, Mobile and Compatibility (Web).
No textures; everything is generated by shaders.

## Install

Copy `addons/pyrolux_fx` into your project and drag a preset from
`addons/pyrolux_fx/presets/3d` into a scene. Open `demo/lite_demo.tscn` to see both
(Space or the on-screen button: explosion, E: fire on / off, H: help).

## Use

```gdscript
$Campfire.ignite()
$Campfire.extinguish()
Pyrolux.spawn(self, "explosion_grenade", hit.position)
$Explosion.explode_at(point)
```

Presets: `fire_campfire`, `fire_torch`, `explosion_grenade`. The fire's `shape`, `size`,
`flame_height`, colours (`palette`) and the explosion's `radius` and parts are all editable.

## The full pack

PYROLUX adds 10 3D effect types and 5 2D types (with pixel-art mode): bonfires, walls and
rings of fire, coloured fire, mushroom clouds, air bursts, napalm, smoke columns, sparks,
embers and ash, lava pools and rivers, flamethrowers and thrusters, meteors, scorch marks,
and PyroluxBurn3D, which sets any mesh on fire and lets it char or burn away.
49 presets, 6 palettes, a browser demo and a click-to-explode playground.
